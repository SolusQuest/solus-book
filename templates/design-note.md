# Design note template

Use the fields needed for the decision. Replace guidance with the actual result and omit irrelevant sections. Follow [Design refinement](../skills/design-refinement/SKILL.md); a design note does not add an approval stage.

## Decision and context

State the concrete choice, affected consumer, current behavior, and accepted constraints. Identify the decision owner and whether the result is proposed or accepted.

## Recommendation and alternatives

Explain the recommended option, meaningful alternatives, evidence, and practical tradeoffs. Include the cost of a wrong choice when it matters.

## Scope and affected boundaries

Describe the intended outcome and exclusions. Identify affected contracts, ownership, dependencies, compatibility, or side-effect authority only where relevant.

## Acceptance and validation

Describe observable acceptance and checks that can distinguish correct behavior. Separate proposed experiments from completed evidence.

## Open decisions and durable record

List remaining material choices and independent work that can continue. Identify where accepted conclusions will be recorded.
